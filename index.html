Crane tech solutions

Crane tech solutions helps crane operators and site managers keep crane work organised, from the first request to the finished job.

What we offer

  • Work orders: a simple form to record a crane job: customer, site, crane, job type, priority, date and a description.
  • Saved list: orders you enter are kept on this device and listed so you can review them.
  • CSV export: download your saved orders as a spreadsheet-friendly file.

Good to know

Work orders are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing the site data in your browser removes them. Export to CSV to keep a copy.
